The story

I started a trend.
Activision ended it.

I'm Luckey Faraday, and I can't really code. I direct AI agents. Since January 2026 I've built with them nearly every day and published almost all of it: the code, the prompts, the runs that failed and the revenue. Here's how that got me a DMCA.

  1. Aug 29 2026

    I started the AI browser ports.

    I gave a team of AI agents one job: port Black Ops 2 to the browser. I left them looping for 24 hours and it worked. It spread fast, and within weeks AI-built browser versions of Halo and GTA were going around too.

    157K views on the first post

  2. Sep 17 2026

    Then I started game mixing.

    I put Black Ops 2 inside Minecraft. First on a PC you could walk up to in the game, then natively: GPT Astra rewrote the Hijacked map in Java so it ran on Minecraft's own OpenGL context, at 45 fps, as a Fabric mod.

    862K views on one post

  3. Oct 9 2026

    Activision DMCA'd me.

    X withheld 13 of my posts over a copyright notice filed for Activision, and every port went offline. I took the rest down myself. I might be the first vibe coder a major publisher has ever DMCA'd.
